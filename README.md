# 📚 Teacher Rapport Tracker

A mobile-first web app to help students track daily interactions with their teachers and build real relationships — one small action at a time.

---

## What It Does

Track 4 simple actions for each of your 6 classes, every school day:

- 👋 Said hi before class
- 🙏 Said thank you after class
- 💬 Left a comment or thank you
- 🏫 Visited at Smart Period

Color-coded by subject, with a weekly summary showing streaks and totals. Data is saved on your device — nothing is sent anywhere.

---

## Live App

👉 **https://matthewmurawski356.github.io/teacher-tracker/**

Install it on your phone:
1. Open the link above in **Safari**
2. Tap the **Share** button
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**
4. Tap **Add**
